About the NSPCA

The National Council of SPCAs (NSPCA) is a non-profit organisation dedicated to protecting animals from cruelty and promoting animal welfare across South Africa.

Established in 1955, the NSPCA works with local SPCAs, government departments, communities and law enforcement agencies to ensure that animals are treated with care, dignity and respect.

Our Mission

Our mission is to prevent cruelty to animals through law enforcement, education, advocacy and community engagement.

Our Vision

We strive for a South Africa where every animal is protected from cruelty, neglect and abuse.
