Welcome to the NSPCA

The National Council of SPCAs (NSPCA) is dedicated to preventing cruelty to animals and promoting animal welfare across South Africa.

We work with communities, investigate cases of animal cruelty, rescue animals, educate the public, and promote responsible pet ownership.

About the NSPCA

Since 1955, the NSPCA has worked to protect animals through inspections, education, legal action, and public awareness campaigns.
